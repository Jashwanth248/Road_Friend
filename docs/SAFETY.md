# Road-awareness safety boundary

RoadMate AI is a navigation and information project, not an autonomous-driving or certified driver-assistance system.

- Traffic-light/sign perception is advisory/educational only.
- The assistant must not issue an authoritative "go" command based on camera inference.
- A driver must follow physical traffic controls, road conditions, applicable law and their own judgment.
- Camera analysis should be demonstrated in simulation, prerecorded media, or by a passenger/parked user.
